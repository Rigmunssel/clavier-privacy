Data Deletion Request

Clavier (Android app, package com.clavier.piano), developer: Rigmunssel

This page explains how to request deletion of data associated with your use of Clavier. Clavier has no user accounts.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to rgmunssel@gmail.com with the subject "Clavier data deletion request".
  2. Include the Google Play order number of your Clavier subscription (shown in your Google Play receipt email, starting with "GPA."), so that we can find your record.
  3. We will delete the data and confirm by email within 30 days.

Data that is deleted

  • The subscription record held by our subscription provider (RevenueCat): app user ID, purchase history for that ID, and technical information such as app version, device model and country.

Data that may be kept

  • Transaction records that we are legally required to keep for tax and accounting purposes, for the period required by law.
  • Purchase records held by Google under your Google account. These are controlled by Google and can be managed in your Google account.

Data stored on your device

Scores, practice history, settings, calibration clips and imported songs are stored only on your device and are never sent to us. To delete them, uninstall Clavier, or open Android Settings → Apps → Clavier → Storage → Clear storage.

See also the Privacy Policy.
