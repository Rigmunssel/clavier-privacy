Privacy Policy

Clavier (Android app, package com.clavier.piano)
Effective date: 24 November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes what personal data is processed when you use the Clavier mobile application ("Clavier", "the app"), for what purposes, and what rights you have.

1. Data controller and contact

The app is published on Google Play by the developer Rigmunssel ("we", "us"), who is the data controller for the processing described in this policy.

Contact for all privacy matters: rgmunssel@gmail.com

2. Data we collect

Clavier does not require an account. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, or advertising identifier. The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking tools.

The only personal data that leaves your device relates to the subscription:

This data is sent only when the app checks or changes your subscription status. Providing it is necessary to use the subscription; the free part of the app works without a subscription.

3. Data that stays on your device

The following is processed and stored only on your device. It is not transmitted to us or to any third party:

  • Microphone audio. With your permission, the app uses the microphone to detect the notes you play. Audio is analysed in real time on the device and is not recorded, stored or transmitted.
  • Calibration clips. During piano calibration, a short audio clip of each calibration note is saved on the device to improve note detection. Clearing or repeating the calibration deletes these clips.
  • Practice data and settings. Scores, practice history, settings and songs you import.
  • Notifications. If you enable practice reminders, they are scheduled locally on the device.

4. Recipients and service providers

We do not sell personal data and do not share it for advertising. The subscription data in section 2 is processed by the following parties:

  • RevenueCat, Inc. (United States), which provides the subscription management service, acting as our processor. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.
  • Google (Google Play), which processes the purchase and payment as an independent controller. We never receive your payment card or bank details. See the Google Privacy Policy.

We may also disclose data where required by law.

5. International transfers

RevenueCat and Google may process data in the United States and other countries outside the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom. Such transfers are made under appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses included in the providers' data processing terms.

6. Retention

Subscription records are kept for as long as the subscription is active and afterwards for as long as necessary to meet legal, tax and accounting obligations and to resolve disputes. Data stored on your device remains until you delete it, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app.

7. Security

Data sent from the app is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Access to subscription records is restricted to us and our service providers.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access your personal data, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability. Where processing is based on legitimate interests, you may object to it at any time. To exercise these rights, contact us at the address in section 1. Because the app has no accounts, we may ask for your Google Play order number so that we can find your record.

Instructions for deleting your data are on the data deletion page. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in Google Play.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the data protection authority of the country where you live or work.

9. Automated decision-making

We do not use your personal data for automated decision-making or profiling.

10. Children

Clavier is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always available on this page, and the effective date above shows when it was last changed.
